Wiring & circuit
    

  • MQ-3 VCC/heater → 5 V rail; GND → GND rail
    • 

  • RL = 10 kΩ from MQ-3 AO to GND (load resistor)
    • 

  • MQ-3 AO → LM358 pin 3 (+IN)
    • 

  • LM358 pin 8 → 5 V, pin 4 → GND
    • 

  • Non-inverting amplifier: Rf = 4.7 kΩ (pin 1 → pin 2), Rg = 10 kΩ (pin 2 → GND), gain ≈ 1.47
    • 

  • LM358 pin 1 (OUT) → Arduino A0; Arduino 5 V / GND → rails
    • 

  • Pin 13 → 220 Ω → red LED anode; LED cathode → GND
    • 

  • Pin 8 → buzzer (+); buzzer (−) → GND


The LM358 output can’t swing fully to 5 V, so it clips near 3.6 V — watch the reading saturate on heavy breath.


Educational model only. A real MQ-3 needs ~24 h burn-in, warm-up and calibration, and must never be used for legal, medical or driving decisions.
